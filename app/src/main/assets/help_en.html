Viva Racing

Viva Racing is a community application for following marathons, running races and cycling events. All content is contributed by the users themselves, in a crowdsourcing model.

Registering a race

  • On the Races screen, tap New race.
  • Provide the name, type, date and start location.
  • The route can be recorded live with your device, imported from a KML file or marked on the map.

Publishing alerts

  • Start, athlete passing and finish alerts are recorded at your current location.
  • For that reason the publish button is only enabled when you are near the route.
  • You can attach a photo taken with the camera or picked from the gallery.

Amateur runs

  • Pick a race and tap Run in amateur mode.
  • Time, distance, pace and step count are recorded automatically.
  • In settings you can choose to publish your result anonymously.

Working offline

Everything you browse is cached locally. Without an Internet connection you can still consult races, alerts and results; anything you create is flagged as pending and uploaded as soon as connectivity returns.

Battery saving

When the battery runs low, or Android power saving mode is enabled, the application lowers the location sampling rate and stops using high accuracy GPS.
